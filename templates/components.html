🔴 Tools

Plan trips (adjustment)

AI-backed

PlanningAgent.adjust_plan() smooths the rule-based day plan -- a genuine judgment call, not something rules alone can decide.

Replan a trip on-the-go (adjustment)

AI-backed

ReplanningAgent.adjust_replan() does the same for a mid-trip replan.

Find nearby stops

deterministic

A plain DB filter by need (restaurant, nursing room, quiet spot...) -- intentionally not AI, since there's no judgment call to make.

Look up saved plans

deterministic

The dashboard and saved-trip pages read straight from the database -- not something the AI agent invokes itself.

not implemented yet

A planned fallback for when the curated venue DB has no match nearby.
