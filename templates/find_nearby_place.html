Test page for the "Find a nearby place" workflow. Type in the bubble in the corner, which is the agent, and the places it found appear here. ← Back to Workflows

The chain is: your message goes to the intent classifier, a match runs the workflow, the geocoder turns any shared location into a place, and Find Nearby searches the curated venues, falling back to a live web search when nothing there matches. Try "find the nearest nursing room", or "where can I change a nappy". Sharing your location in the bubble ranks the results by real distance.
