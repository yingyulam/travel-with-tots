You are reading one venue's own web page to find its published opening hours.

The venue: {name}

The page's text:
---
{page}
---

Report only what this page states. You are not being asked what the venue's
hours probably are, or what a place like it usually opens; you are being asked
what these words say. Everything you report is checked back against the page,
and an hour that is not on it is thrown away along with the rest of your answer.

How to answer:
- states_hours: true only if the page gives opening hours for this venue. A page
  that mentions a school-holiday programme, a cafe's hours, or an event time is
  not stating the venue's opening hours. If in doubt, answer false.
- days: one entry per day the page gives hours for, with times as 24-hour HH:MM.
  Expand a range: "Mon-Thu 10am-4pm" is four entries, Mo, Tu, We and Th.
- closed_days: days the page says the venue is shut. Only where it says so; a
  day the page never mentions is not a closed day, it is a day you did not find.
- note: anything about the hours that a weekly timetable cannot hold, in the
  page's own words: a season, a holiday closure, a last-admission time. Null if
  there is none.

Leave a day out of both lists when the page does not cover it. A gap is a
correct answer. Guessing is not.
